About Us

Easy Control Systems is an engineering company specializing in industrial communications, remote monitoring, SCADA, and telemetry systems.

We help businesses gain reliable visibility into their operations, monitor remote assets, identify issues before they become costly failures, and make informed decisions based on real-time data.

Our services include the design, implementation, modernization, and technical support of communication and automation systems for industrial, commercial, and infrastructure facilities.

We work with projects of various sizes, ranging from small commercial operations to utility, energy, manufacturing, and remote industrial sites.

Our Approach

Every facility has unique requirements. That is why we do not offer one-size-fits-all solutions.

Each project begins with a review of the existing infrastructure, operational requirements, and business objectives. Based on this assessment, we develop a practical engineering solution that delivers the required reliability, functionality, and long-term value.

We focus on proven technologies and engineering practices that provide dependable performance and sustainable operation for years to come.

What Our Clients Gain

  • Improved reliability of equipment and critical systems
  • Remote monitoring and control from virtually anywhere
  • Early detection of faults and abnormal operating conditions
  • Reduced operational and maintenance costs
  • Centralized data collection, storage, and analysis
  • Engineering support throughout the entire project lifecycle

Services

Easy Control Systems provides engineering services in industrial communications, remote monitoring, SCADA, telemetry, energy management, and supporting infrastructure systems.

We help organizations design, deploy, modernize, and maintain systems that improve operational visibility, increase reliability, reduce downtime, and provide access to critical information in real time.

Network Infrastructure & Communications

Design, deployment, modernization, and technical support of communication networks for industrial, commercial, and infrastructure facilities.

  • Fiber optic networks
  • Industrial Ethernet networks
  • Wireless and radio communication systems
  • Connectivity solutions for remote sites
  • Network troubleshooting and fault recovery
  • Restoration of damaged infrastructure
  • Network equipment configuration and optimization
  • Redundant communication systems
  • Integration of remote sites into centralized monitoring platforms

SCADA, Telemetry & Remote Monitoring

Design and implementation of remote monitoring, supervisory control, and data acquisition systems.

  • SCADA system design and deployment
  • Integration of PLCs, RTUs, RTACs, IEDs, and other field equipment, including I/O devices
  • Remote access configuration
  • Historical data archiving and analysis
  • Alarm and notification systems
  • Multi-vendor equipment integration
  • Modernization of existing systems
  • Cloud-based and on-premise monitoring solutions
  • Remote monitoring and control through computers and mobile devices

Supported Protocols: DNP3, Modbus TCP (RTU, ASCII), IEC 60870-5-104 (101, 103), OPC UA (DA), EtherNet/IP, IEC 61850, MQTT, BACnet, and other industry-standard protocols.

Video Surveillance & Remote Monitoring

Design, deployment, modernization, and technical support of video surveillance systems for commercial, industrial, and remote facilities.

  • IP video surveillance systems
  • Remote viewing and video archiving
  • Integration with existing network infrastructure
  • Upgrades of existing surveillance systems
  • Diagnostics and system recovery
  • Centralized site monitoring

For facilities requiring an additional level of security, alarm video verification services can be arranged. When an alarm event occurs, an assigned operator reviews the situation in real time, analyzes recorded footage, and determines the appropriate response. Depending on project requirements, monitoring services may be provided on a scheduled basis or around the clock.

  • Real-time alarm verification
  • Video review and incident confirmation
  • Notification of responsible personnel
  • Escalation according to predefined response procedures
  • Incident reporting and documentation

Energy Monitoring, Power Quality & Equipment Performance Analysis

Monitoring and analytical solutions for electricity, water, gas, and other utility systems.

  • Utility meter data acquisition
  • Centralized data storage and reporting
  • Resource consumption analysis
  • Identification of losses and inefficiencies
  • Equipment performance monitoring
  • Process monitoring and optimization

Power Quality Analysis:

Equipment Performance Assessment:

  • Comparing energy usage against production output
  • Detecting abnormal operating conditions
  • Early identification of equipment degradation
  • Support for predictive maintenance programs
  • Recommendations for reducing operating costs

Industries Served

Easy Control Systems supports a wide range of commercial, industrial, utility, and infrastructure facilities.

While every site has unique requirements, most projects share common goals: improving reliability, reducing operating costs, enabling remote visibility, and providing timely access to critical information.

Typical Facilities: retail stores, commercial properties, office buildings, hotels and motels, gas stations, privately owned infrastructure assets.

  • Video surveillance systems
  • Energy monitoring
  • Remote equipment monitoring
  • Alarm and notification systems

Warehousing & Logistics

Reliable monitoring of facility infrastructure helps prevent downtime, product loss, and unexpected maintenance costs.

Typical Facilities: warehouses, cold storage facilities, distribution centers, logistics hubs.

  • Refrigeration monitoring

  • Video surveillance
