About Us

We are an engineering team specializing in telemechanics and industrial automation systems.

Our mission is to enhance business operational efficiency by optimizing the use of energy and human resources.

How We Work

We operate on a model based on a preliminary comprehensive audit. The initial audit is free of charge. Payment for our services begins only after the audit is completed and you decide to implement the proposed solutions.

We eliminate unjustified risks and experimental approaches in the financial side of projects. All decisions are made on the basis of technical analysis, calculations, and proven practical experience.

Based on the initial assessment, we determine:

  • project feasibility;
  • economic efficiency;
  • estimated payback period.

Competencies and Regulatory Compliance

Our company has an expanding set of licenses and competencies, allowing us to handle a wide range of engineering tasks. Before starting any project, our legal department performs an additional review to ensure compliance with regulatory requirements.

Alarm Video Verification — Live Operator Monitoring

  • When motion is detected or other critical alerts occur after hours, our operator reviews the event in real time
  • Analyzes the video archive and confirms or dismisses the alarm
  • If false alarm — the system is reset, no police dispatch
  • If a genuine intrusion is confirmed — the operator immediately notifies emergency services with the exact location and incident description

Benefits of Video Verification

  • No fines for false police dispatches (saving up to $500 per incident)
  • Fast response to real threats
  • 24/7 monitoring
  • Legally admissible incident evidence
  • Compliance with alarm video verification standard (AVL-01)

Additional Services

  • Complete video surveillance system maintenance — scheduled and emergency
  • Upon request (for incident investigation) — fast retrieval of archived footage with timestamp and export in required format
